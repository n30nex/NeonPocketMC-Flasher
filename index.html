STEP 4

Restart, but stay connected

The firmware bytes are verified. Now prove the device comes back over USB before configuration.

  1. Keep USB connected. Do not unplug or move the device yet.
  2. Press Reset once. For RC52, wait until the UF2 drive disappears and the normal serial device returns.
  3. Check the screen or status LED. A screen build should show the NeonPocket startup and then its home/status page.
